Warm floor spots, hissing sounds or a water bill that jumped?

Those are the signs people notice before they know the term. A hot spot on the floor, the sound of water when everything is off or a sudden jump in the utility bill often points to a slab leak. That is a supply line leaking in or beneath the concrete foundation slab.

A slab leak is a plumbing failure and a water damage loss at the same time. A plumber locates and repairs the line. The restoration crew deals with what the water soaked.

What a slab leak is and why it matters

Many Florida homes are built slab-on-grade, which means supply and drain lines can run in or under the concrete. A leaking line below the slab wets the soil and rises through cracks and joints, or soaks the underside of flooring from below. If your home has a raised foundation or crawl space, a leak is a different problem and is covered on the crawl space page.

Because the water starts out of sight, slab leaks are often found late. The damage can include warped flooring, damp baseboards, musty odor and, in time, mold. Quick action limits that.

Water on the floor from a slab leak is an emergency

If you suspect a slab leak, do these first.

Close the main valve

If the meter keeps moving after every fixture is off, a leak is likely. Closing the main stops further flow.

Look for wet spots

Check flooring, baseboards and rugs for dampness, warm spots or lifting planks.

Keep power safe

Do not stand in water near outlets or appliances. Call the utility or 911 for electrical risk first.

Call a plumber and this line

A plumber finds and repairs the leak. We handle water removal and drying.

Finding the leak and drying the structure

Plumbers use acoustic listening gear, pressure tests and thermal imaging to find a slab leak. Repair options include rerouting the line through walls or ceiling, or breaking the concrete to repair the line. That is the plumber's decision.

On the water damage side, crews remove wet flooring, extract water and run air movers and dehumidifiers. Some floors can be dried in place with specialized drying systems. The structural drying page explains the equipment.

What affects slab leak water damage costs

The plumbing repair and the water damage cleanup are separate bills.

  • DurationA leak found early wets less than one running for weeks.
  • Floor finishTile, wood, laminate and carpet respond differently.
  • Area affectedWater can spread under a large part of the house.
  • Mold riskLong-term moisture raises the chance of mold. See mold remediation.
  • Drying methodIn-place systems or removal.

Insurance coverage for slab leaks varies by policy. Some policies cover resulting damage but not the repair of the pipe itself. Check your policy and ask your insurer. We explain scope and price before work starts.

What else a leak under the floor can affect

Once the leak is found, the damage above it is the work of restoration. Wet floors are handled on the hardwood floor and carpet pages, and the water extraction page explains how surface water is removed.

Safety steps while you wait

Shut off the main water valve and keep people off wet floors near outlets. Do not break into the slab yourself. A plumber locates and repairs the leak.

The published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard then guides drying of the wet floor and walls once the leak is repaired.

Slab foundations and Dunedin plumbing

Many Florida homes, including many in Pinellas County, are built on concrete slabs, which puts plumbing in or near the concrete. If a leak develops, the water has few places to go but up, and in a neighborhood of similar homes the same age, one slab leak is a reason to ask a plumber about the rest of the lines.

The City of Dunedin Water Division reads your meter and can help you check usage. If a leak is on the city side of the meter, the utility handles it.
