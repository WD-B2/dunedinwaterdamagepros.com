Water damage in Clearwater, handled from Dunedin

The Dunedin Water Damage Pros line serves Clearwater homes and businesses around the clock, and the same emergency number reaches the dispatcher whether you are in Clearwater, Dunedin or a nearby town such as Safety Harbor.

When water is spreading in Clearwater

If water is still moving, stop the source first. Close the shutoff for the leaking fixture or the main valve, and call. Emergency water removal is available around the clock in Clearwater, including nights, weekends and holidays, and the dispatcher will ask for the address, the source and the rooms that are wet.

Clearwater has waterfront neighborhoods along the Gulf and the Intracoastal side, and low-lying streets can collect water in heavy rain. Flood losses and storm losses follow different steps, which the flood damage cleanup and storm water damage pages explain.

Clearwater at a glance

These facts shape how a water damage repair goes here.

Emergency dispatch

The line is open around the clock, so a Clearwater call at night gets answered.

County and city offices

Clearwater is in Pinellas County, but it has its own city government, so permit questions go to the City of Clearwater, not the City of Dunedin.

Building permits

The City of Clearwater says a building permit is required to alter or repair a building, with exceptions such as painting and carpet or tile installation. Permits are applied for mainly through its online Accela system.

Utilities

Clearwater has its own Public Utilities department, separate from the City of Dunedin Water Division, so a main-line problem goes to a different office.

Roads and routes into Clearwater

The usual drive from Dunedin to Clearwater is south on US 19 Alt or Edgewater Drive, or east and south via SR 580. Clearwater Beach sits on a barrier island reached by the Memorial Causeway, and a storm or heavy traffic can slow that route.

If your property is on the beach side, mention it when you call so the dispatcher can plan the route and access.

What Clearwater callers ask about

Callers in Clearwater commonly describe ceilings stained after a roof leak, supply line and appliance leaks, water heater failures, and standing water after storms. For each, the first step is to stop the water, and the second is to get extraction started.

The pages that cover those causes are water extraction, ceiling water damage, burst pipe damage and structural drying. Mold questions after a leak are on the mold remediation page.

What shapes the cost of a Clearwater job

No honest company can name a number before it sees the water. These factors set the scope.

  • Water categoryClean supply water costs less to handle than contaminated water, which needs added protection and disposal.
  • Area and materialsThe number of rooms, the square footage and whether carpet, wood or drywall is wet all change the scope.
  • Drying daysEquipment runs until readings show dry, so humidity and building materials affect the number of days.
  • Rebuild scopeRemoved drywall, flooring and cabinets add repair work after drying, and that work is separate from mitigation.

Your policy sets what an insurer covers, so check your policy and ask your insurer. We explain scope and price before work starts.
