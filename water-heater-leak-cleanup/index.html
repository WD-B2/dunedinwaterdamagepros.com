Why a water heater leak is different

A tank water heater stores 40 to 80 gallons or more. When the tank corrodes through or a fitting fails, that stored volume can empty onto a floor within minutes, and then the supply line keeps feeding it. That makes a water heater leak a quick, sizable loss rather than a slow drip.

This page is about the heater itself as the source. A burst supply line elsewhere in the house is on the burst pipe page, and a leaking appliance such as a washer or dishwasher is on the appliance leak page.

Shut the heater down safely

Gas and electric heaters need different first steps.

Electric heater

Turn off the breaker for the heater if you can reach the panel on dry ground. Do not touch the panel if the floor around it is wet.

Gas heater

Turn the gas control to pilot or off. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 first.

Cold water inlet

Close the shutoff valve on the cold water line above the heater, or close the main.

Relief valve discharge

A pipe running from the temperature and pressure relief valve that is dripping can signal a different problem. Mention it to the plumber.

Once the heater is isolated, call the 24/7 line so extraction can begin.

When a leaking heater is an emergency

If water is pooling, spreading under walls or reaching carpet, call right away. Heaters often sit in garages, closets or laundry rooms, where water spreads along the slab and under adjoining walls.

Florida homes commonly place heaters in garages, so water can reach the garage door seal and spread outdoors, or into the interior through a shared wall. Drywall at the bottom of that wall wicks water upward, so quick extraction matters.

Cleanup after a water heater failure

Crews extract standing water, check moisture in walls and flooring and remove materials that cannot be dried. Wet cardboard, stored items and carpet pad are usually the first to go. Hard floors and concrete dry well when treated quickly.

Drying uses air movers and dehumidifiers, with readings taken until the structure is dry. Our structural drying page explains how that works.

What shapes water heater cleanup costs

The loss size and materials set the scope.

  • Water volumeA full tank spreads wider than a small leak.
  • LocationGarage slabs dry differently than carpeted closets.
  • MaterialsCarpet, drywall and stored items drive removal.
  • Drying timeEquipment runs until readings return to normal.
  • Heater replacementThe plumber or installer handles the new heater.

Sudden tank failures are often covered by homeowners insurance, while wear and neglect over time often are not. Check your policy and ask your insurer. We explain scope and price before work starts.

Standards behind this page

Drying after a heater leak follows the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard. A plumber handles the heater itself, and a licensed electrician should check any wiring that got wet.
