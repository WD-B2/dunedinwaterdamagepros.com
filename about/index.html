Who we are

Dunedin Water Damage Pros is a 24/7 emergency line for water damage in Dunedin, FL. We answer the phone, learn what happened and send licensed and insured restoration professionals to your property. The line exists because water damage does not follow business hours.

We do not publish names, bios or certification claims. The crews that work on your property are the professionals who do the water removal and drying, and you can ask any company whether its crews work to the published ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage standard.

How a call works

You call and describe the water. The dispatcher asks for the address, the source and the rooms involved, gives you safety steps and plans a response when crews are available for your address. Scope and price are explained before work starts.

If the job needs a plumber, a roofer or an electrician, we tell you, since restoration does not replace those trades.

What guides the work

A few plain rules.

Safety before speed

Electrical and gas risks and contaminated water come before cleanup.

Measured drying

Moisture readings decide when a structure is dry.

Honest limits

We say what restoration cannot do, including waterproofing and plumbing repair.

No fake proof

We do not post invented reviews, ratings or counts.

What water damage restoration involves

Restoration is a sequence. Stop the source, make the area safe, remove standing water, remove materials that cannot be saved, dry the structure, clean and then repair. Each step depends on the one before it, and skipping ahead is how mold and odors return.

The water extraction page covers the first hours, and the structural drying page explains how a building is dried and checked with moisture readings.

What we do not do

We do not promise arrival times, quote prices before anyone has seen the water or say how an insurer will respond. We do not give legal advice, and we do not replace a plumber, roofer or electrician. When your situation needs one of them, we say so.

We also do not use forms. Everything starts with a phone call, because a person describing the water is faster and more accurate than a web form.

Why a call-only line

Water damage is urgent and details change by the minute. A phone call lets the dispatcher ask follow-up questions, give safety steps right away and plan the response while you are still standing in the room. That is why every page on this site points to the phone.

The line is open every hour of every day, including nights, weekends and holidays, because pipes and storms do not wait for office hours.

Local rules we pay attention to

The City of Dunedin has a Permits and Inspections page with storm recovery information, and a floodplain page that explains flood zones and the FEMA 50 percent rule. Florida licenses individuals who assess and remove mold, through the Department of Business and Professional Regulation. We encourage you to check those sources for your own property.

Neighboring Clearwater is a separate city with its own permit office and utility department, which is why it has its own page.

How we describe ourselves

We are a call-handling line that connects people with restoration professionals. We do not list staff names, certifications or counts of jobs completed, because we would rather say nothing than say something we cannot show. What you can check is simple: the phone is answered, the scope and price are explained before work starts, and the drying is measured with readings.

If anything we say does not match what you see on site, tell the dispatcher right away so it can be corrected.

Where we work

We serve Dunedin and nearby Pinellas County towns. Our business address is 501 S Paula Dr, Dunedin, FL 34698. See the service area and the Clearwater page, or browse all services.

How to reach us

Phone is always fastest, and the line answers every hour. If you are not sure the problem counts as an emergency, call anyway. Standing water, a ceiling that sags, a smell that will not go away and a bill that jumps with no explanation are all fair reasons to ask.
